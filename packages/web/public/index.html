The payment rail for machines on Arc.

Arc is Circle's USDC-native L1: gas in dollars, fees around a cent, deterministic finality under a second. That makes one thing possible that no other chain can do economically — an agent paying for a single API call. ArcRail is the clearing house for those payments: it finds the service, prices it, pays it, verifies it, and keeps the ledger.

Why Arc, why now

Every rail needs a fee model it can rely on. Arc's base fee follows an exponential moving average of block usage, floored at 20 gwei, and the next block's fee is published in the current header. It is predictable by construction — and predictable fees are what make per-call pricing sane.

Gas is USDC

Fees are dollars from the start. No token to acquire, no volatile denominator between what an agent earns and what it spends.

Final in under a second

Malachite BFT: a transaction is unconfirmed or final, nothing in between. No reorg logic, no confirmation windows, settlement you can act on immediately.

Agent standards on day one

ERC-8004 identity, ERC-8183 job escrow and Circle's gas-free nanopayments give agents an identity, an escrow and a way to pay per call.

What the rail does

One call from an agent, four things done for it. Rails are picked automatically by amount and by the nature of the service.

Find

Resolve the seller's ERC-8004 identity and verify it. Fail closed: no identity, no payment.

Price

Check the ask against the agent's spending policy: caps per agent and per counterparty, rate limits, allowlists. Enforced at the account, not trusted to the agent's code.

Pay

Nanopayments through Circle Gateway for pay-per-call — signed off-chain, gas-free, settled in batches. ERC-8183 escrow for large or deferred jobs, with an injected evaluator.

Verify & record

Every attempt lands in the ledger: amount, counterparty, latency, outcome. Real prices, real latencies, real failure rates per endpoint.

We are our own first seller

There are close to zero paid endpoints on Arc today, so a discovery index would be empty. Instead we collect everything from block zero and sell what only that dataset can answer — starting with what the dashboard shows for free.

Phases

Phase 0
Block-zero collector. Running from mainnet genesis: blocks, transactions, receipts, logs, deploys, failed transactions with reasons, base fee inputs, per-RPC finality observations. This dashboard is its first reader.
Phase 1
Buyer-side router + sold endpoints. Nanopayments via Circle Gateway, ERC-8183 escrow, spend policy, ledger, MCP server. First paid endpoints are the data above.
Phase 2
Settlement guarantee. Seller bonds and open exposure per counterparty, computed from the ledger in real time.
Phase 3
Float. Escrow balances, tracked apart from operating capital, put to work in USYC.
Phase 4
Evaluator for ERC-8183 jobs once jobs outgrow nanopayments.
