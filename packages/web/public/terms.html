1. What we offer

  • Data by the call. Routes under /v1/paid and /v1/direct return Arc chain data, executed prices from swaps on Arc and public sources as JSON, for a price per call. Routes under /v1 are free.
  • The thinking agent at /v1/upto/think, which answers a task by paying for its own model calls and tools.
  • An x402 facilitator that settles payments for sellers, on the networks listed at api.cra-agent.tech/facilitator/supported.
  • Open-source software: the CRA AGENT packages on npm and the code at github.com/giupy997/arcagentx402, under the MIT licence.

There are no accounts. Agents and people pay per call, in USDC, over x402.

2. Prices and payment

  • A call costs the price in its 402 response at the moment you pay. Prices are also listed at /.well-known/x402 and in openapi.json. We can change prices at any time; a change never applies to a payment you have already signed.
  • You can pay through Circle Gateway on Arc, with a plain x402 payment on Base or Solana settled by Coinbase's facilitator, or directly on Arc. Those networks and services belong to others and come with their own terms.
  • A payment is settled only when the call succeeds. A bad parameter (400), a source that fails (502) or any other error is not charged.
  • On /v1/upto/think you sign for a ceiling, today $0.10, and are charged what the run spent plus a fee, today $0.005, never more than the ceiling. If the run cannot start, nothing is charged.
  • A settled payment is a transfer on a public blockchain, and we cannot reverse it. If you think you were charged for a call that failed, write to us with the transaction and the route. If we got it wrong, we refund the amount to the address that paid.
  • Every payment our routes verify is listed publicly at cra-agent.tech/status. The Privacy Policy says what that list shows.
