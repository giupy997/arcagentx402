How to read this

Whose wallet. “self-test” is our own wallet buying our own endpoint every hour to prove the rail is alive. “ours” is any other wallet we control. Neither is a customer, and the counts say so separately. “external” is everyone else.

Rail. “direct” is settled one payment at a time by our facilitator, which pays the gas: the hash is there at once. “gateway” is batched by Circle Gateway. “base” is the same routes paid on Base.

Not charged. The payment was valid, the request failed on our side, and the authorization was dropped unused. Try the route that fails on purpose to put a row like that here yourself.
