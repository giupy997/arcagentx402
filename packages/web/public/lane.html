How the lane works, and what is not built yet

Live today. A seller on CRA gives a Solana address and the same routes go on sale to buyers on Solana, in the same 402. The buyer pays USDC on Solana, an open facilitator completes the transaction and covers its fee, and the seller is paid on Solana. Nothing crosses chains and nobody holds anything in between.

Not yet. Moving what sellers earn on Solana to Arc. The road we picked is Eco Routes, an intent network where a solver delivers on Arc and is repaid from a vault on Solana: no float of ours, no bridge to babysit. Eco is live on Arc, and quotes Solana → Base and Base → Arc in about seven seconds each. Solana → Arc has no solver yet: this page asks for a real quote every hour and shows Eco's answer above. When it opens, this is where the figures will appear.

Honest count. Payments from our own wallets are listed on the status page as ours. The first payment on this lane was one of them.
