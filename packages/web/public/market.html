If you run an API, one command puts a price on it without touching its code, and buyers pay you in USDC on Arc. CRA Factory writes the command for you.

Before you pay anyone here. We check that a listing answers and what it charges, not what it sells. Your wallet or your agent shows the amount and the address before anything moves, and a call that fails is not charged. Set your agent's limits accordingly: the Factory does that too.
